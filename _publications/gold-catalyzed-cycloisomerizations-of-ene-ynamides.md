The gold-catalyzed cycloisomerizations of 1,6-ene-ynamides proceed under mild conditions and lead to cyclobutanones from terminal or trimethylsilyl Substituted ynamides, or to carbonyl compounds bearing a 2,3-methanopyrrolidine Subunit from Substrates possessing a propargylic alcohol moiety. High diastereoselectivities are observed with 1,6-ene-ynamides having a stereocenter at the or alpha or beta position of the nitrogen atom. (C) 2008 Elsevier Ltd. All rights reserved.
