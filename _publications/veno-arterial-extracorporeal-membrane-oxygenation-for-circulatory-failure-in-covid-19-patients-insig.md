We identified factors associated with poor prognosis and suggest that E-CPR is not a reasonable indication for VA-ECMO in this population. Since the initial months of the Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) outbreak, extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) has been used in the treatment of severe acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS).
