The present study reports the publications issued in international, peer-reviewed journals by the French University physicians specialised in Anaesthesia, Critical Care and Peri Operative Medicine between the 1st of January 2010 and the 5th of May 2021. Methods > From the French SIGAPS database assessing medical publications, the publications authored by the University physicians belonging to the French College of Anaesthesia and Critical Care Teachers (CNEAR) were searched and analysed according to their rank (A to E), year of publication and position within the authors list. Results > Seven thousand six hundred and forty-four articles were published by 181 university physicians affiliated to the CNEAR: 3,724 (48%) in rank A (23%) or rank B (25%) journals. These articles were mainly published in intensive care medicine (2,821; 37%) or anaesthesiology (2437; 32%) specialised journals. Fifty-four articles were published in high-rank (called ``A+'') general medical journals (Journal of theAmericanMedicalAssociation [n = 25], New EnglandJournal of Medicine [n = 16] and The Lancet [n = 13]). The median number of publications per physician was 51 (minimal = 4, Interquartile [20-71], maximal = 417]. In 5199 published articles (68% of total), the CNEAR physician was in 1st (n = 1685), 2nd (n = 519), penultimate (n = 608) or last (n = 2387) position. This proportion of ``significant'' position within the authors list was 62%, when considering rank A journals only. Altogether, these published articles gained 106,732 SIGAPS points. Conclusion > University physicians belonging to the CNEAR published half of their articles in rank A or B journals, mainly in first, second, penultimate or last positions. The current assessment should be continued prospectively and encourage collaborative national and international multicentre trials to increase the proportion of articles published in rank A+ journals.
