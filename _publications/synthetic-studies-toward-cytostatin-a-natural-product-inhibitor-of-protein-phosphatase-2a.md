Synthetic approaches toward the natural product cytostatin, an inhibitor of protein phosphatase 2A possessing cytotoxic and antimetastatic activities, have been investigated. A formal synthesis of cytostatin has been achieved according to a strategy relying on the formation of the C8-C9 bond by a nucleophilic addition of a functionalized organolithium (C1-C8 subunit) to an aldehyde (C9-C13 subunit).
