By detecting subtle afterloader malfunctions, the 7SD offers a precise and valuable means of evaluating the integrity of HDR-BT setups and procedures. Its high-resolution monitoring capabilities, confirmed biocompatibility, scalability, and compatibility with standard BT needles, catheters, and applicators underscore its potential for clinical implementation."
