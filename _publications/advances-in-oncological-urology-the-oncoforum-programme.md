Objectives: This review paper presents the results of the Oncoforum project: a selection of the major key points on prostate cancer (PCa) presented during urological and oncological 2006 annual meetings. Methods: The most interesting data on PCa were selected and presented during a satellite symposium held at the European Association of Urology (EAU) 2007 annual meeting. Results: A major topic related to diagnosis of PCa concerned the observation that a consensus exists that a distinct PSA threshold for the early detection of clinically relevant PCa cannot yet be identified. However, patients with a PSA 8-50 ng/ml and a PSA doubling time < 12 mo are at high risk of progression and death due to PCa and may need immediate hormone treatment. With regards to hormone-refractory PCa, it was shown that repeat treatment with low-dose docetaxel may be effective in patients who have a PSA relapse after initial docetaxel treatment. Conclusions: This selection of key data on PCa presented at the 2006 oncological and urological congresses highlight a continuing interest in the diagnosis and treatment of PCa.
