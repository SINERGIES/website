In this paper, we propose a new 60 GHz RoF millimeter wave generation system based on Bragg filters. The signal is obtained by the beat of Bragg filters which form with the coupler a Y system. The simulation results have shown that we can have a good quality factor and an optimal bit error ratio (BER) for a 5 Gbit/s flow rate with a very precise choice of reflected bands of Bragg filters, in addition the eye diagram of the generated 60 GHz signal remains open and clear for distances that can reach 50 km and more for a standard single-mode fiber (SSMF).
