This did not translate into a negative impact on BCR, although follow-up was short and many men harbored low-risk PCa. We found that NVB preservation was poorly defined but, if applied, was associated with a higher risk of cancer at the margins of the tissue removed, even in patients with low-risk prostate cancer.
