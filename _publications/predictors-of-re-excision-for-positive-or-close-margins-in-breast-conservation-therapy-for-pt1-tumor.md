Because breast-conservation therapy (BCT) is the standard treatment, the aim of our study was to determine factors predisposing patients to re-excision for pT1 tumors. CONCLUSIONS: These predictive factors could be useful in reducing the rate of re-excision as well as in identifying patients with multifocal tumors and/or extensive in situ carcinoma whose disease would be better managed by mastectomy.
